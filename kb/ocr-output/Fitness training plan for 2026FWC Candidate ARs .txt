From 27 April to 29 May 2026

Tips:







This training plan has been organized for a typical week that involves only one match
on the weekend. If you have another match or a mid-week match – please discuss this
plan with your local coach and adjust for you needs.
We recommend that all training sessions are supervised by your local physical
instructor.
Give special attention to injury prevention exercises in each training session.
If you have any muscular complaints or any form of injury – do not resume a normal
training session – contact your local physiotherapist and fitness coach.
Take care about regular recovery program (massage etc.)
During all training sessions, take care to use proper running technique.
